# brief-me

Makes your AI report like a good chief of staff: verdict first, one decision at a time, no narration of its homework.

**Works with any AI, not just Claude.** A skill is just a set of written rules. Share it with your AI of choice and ask it to fit the skill to you; it will help you edit and revise it until the rules are yours.

The skill itself: [skills/brief-me/SKILL.md](skills/brief-me/SKILL.md)

The story behind it: [Skill "Brief-Me": I Taught My AI to Brief Me Like a CEO](https://cairoglyphics.ai/posts/brief-me-like-a-ceo/)

## Install

**Claude Code:**

```
/plugin marketplace add Cairo-Labs/skills
/plugin install brief-me@cairo-skills
```

**Claude app (web, desktop or phone):** download [brief-me.zip](https://github.com/Cairo-Labs/skills/releases/download/brief-me-v1.0.0/brief-me.zip) and upload it in the Claude app's Skills settings.

**ChatGPT, Gemini or any other AI:** open [SKILL.md](skills/brief-me/SKILL.md), copy everything below the top block (the lines between the `---` markers), and paste it into your custom instructions or a project's instructions.
